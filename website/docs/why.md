Most café owners try three things before they find us. Here's why each one falls short — and what CaféMeeple does differently.

## The spreadsheet trap

Spreadsheets get you started but never scale. You can't show staff a real-time floor plan, you can't enforce capacity on a reservation, and your "popular games" column is always six months stale. CaféMeeple gives you the rigour of a real data model without the overhead of building one.

## Why not Square or Toast?

Generic POS systems are excellent at one thing: selling drinks and food. They are **terrible** at:

- Treating a game as a borrowable asset with a condition history.
- Charging by table-time instead of by item.
- Surfacing alerts that aren't about inventory shrinkage.

You can shoehorn cover charges in as a "service item", but you'll never get the floor-plan view or the checkout/return workflow.

## Why not OpenTable?

Reservation widgets are great at filling seats. They don't know what to do **after** the guest arrives. CaféMeeple is what runs the rest of the night.

## What CaféMeeple won't do

We're honest about scope:

- **No payment processing.** You already have a POS that does this well. CaféMeeple computes the cover charge; settling it is up to your existing system.
- **No food and drinks.** Order food from your POS. CaféMeeple is for the parts your POS can't help with.
- **No marketing automation.** No mailing lists, no SMS blasts. Bring your own tools.

This is by design. CaféMeeple does one thing — runs the operations of a board game café — and refuses to dilute that.

## Pricing

CaféMeeple is **MIT licensed and self-hosted**. Run it on a $5 VPS, run it on your laptop. There is no Pro tier. The team behind it offers paid support if you want it, but the software itself is free forever.
